# How to play

One person opens Couchcade on a TV or laptop. Everyone else scans the QR code or opens the address shown and types the four letter code. No accounts and no app.

The first phone to join becomes the host and can start games, add bots, kick or mute people. The TV screen has the same buttons.

Eight people get a seat. Anyone after that joins as audience and votes in the games that have votes.

| Game | Players | Rounds | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Squiggle | 2 to 8 | 3 | Everyone draws the prompt in secret on the phone. Then everyone votes on the favorite. You cannot vote for your own drawing. |
| Fibber | 2 to 8 | 3 | Write a fake answer to a question. Then pick which answer is real. Fooling people scores. |
| Brain Teams | 2 to 8 | 8 | Two teams answer multiple choice questions. Faster correct answers score more. |
| Zap | 2 to 8 | 5 | Wait for green, then tap. Tapping early costs points. |
| Echo | 2 to 8 | 5 | Everyone answers the same prompt. Matching another player scores. |
| Mole | 3 to 8 | 3 | Everyone but one player sees a secret word. Talk about it, then find the player who does not know it. |

## Family mode

The TV has a Family toggle. When it is on, prompts rated `mature` in a content pack are skipped.

## Reconnecting

A phone that loses signal comes back with the same name and score. The browser keeps a token for the room, so reloading the page is fine.

## Languages

The interface and the prompts follow one language switch on the TV: English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese. Phones follow the TV.
